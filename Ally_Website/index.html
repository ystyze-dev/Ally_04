An object with a need of its own

A breeze shaped byyour words.

Ally is a fan that listens for heat, gives air, and asks for breath in return. A functional object becomes an extension of the body—and a relationship of mutual need.

Language becomes air.

Speak what is warm, burning, humid, or dry. Your words gather into a breeze; the object listens to language, not volume.

A proposition for reciprocity

An exchange between object and subject

I gave you the wind.
Now lend me your breath.

Ally does not only serve. It expresses absence, desire, and the need for air. Its poem is not an instruction; it is an invitation to come closer.

I have given you air.
Ahora préstame tu aliento.

Air moves both ways.

Speech becomes motion; silence makes room for a voice; breath completes the exchange.
