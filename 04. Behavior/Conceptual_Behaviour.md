
## Conceptual behaviour

The object as an extension of the subject

Ally is conceived as an extension of the human subject rather than as an autonomous domestic appliance.

Its behaviour emerges from the subject's language, bodily presence and participation.

The object externalizes the thermal content of human discourse by translating language into airflow. Physical movement becomes a manifestation of linguistic expression.

Heat is not understood exclusively as a measurable temperature. It is also understood as an emotional, erotic, environmental, material and symbolic condition.

The object responds to the multiple manifestations of heat embedded within human language.

## From function to relationship

A conventional fan responds to a human need through a predetermined function: the production of airflow.

AERA extends this function by establishing a relationship in which the object is also capable of expressing a need.

During silence, the object interrupts its condition of service and becomes an expressive entity.

Through poetry, it communicates an apparent vulnerability and a desire for air.

## Mutual dependence

The subject depends on the object for the production of airflow.

The object, through its programmed behaviour, depends on the subject's breath to complete its interaction cycle.

This dependence is relational and symbolic rather than a literal biological necessity.

The object becomes an extension of the subject, while the subject becomes an active extension of the object's operation.

## Research intention

Ally investigates how computational behaviour can transform a utilitarian object into a relational object through language, physical sensation and reciprocal gestures.
