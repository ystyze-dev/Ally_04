
Physical behaviour

## Language as movement

The physical behaviour of Ally is determined by the thermal content of human speech.

Each recognized heat-related reference contributes to the accumulation of linguistic heat.

This accumulation is translated into rotational movement and airflow.

## States of movement

Stillness

The object remains physically inactive while maintaining its capacity to listen.

Accumulation

Heat-related language initiates movement.

Intensification

The continued presence of thermal references increases the intensity of airflow.

Saturation

The object reaches its maximum programmed rotational speed.

Dissipation

When thermal references disappear, the fan gradually decreases its speed.

Absence

The object becomes still.

The absence of movement prepares the transition from physical expression to linguistic expression.

Reception

The object remains still while receiving the subject's breath.

Renewal

After the reciprocal exchange, the object returns to its receptive state.

Air as a medium

Air is the physical medium through which the object communicates with the human body.

It is simultaneously an output produced by the object and an input provided by the subject.

Through this exchange, air becomes a shared material rather than a unilateral functional product.
