
Reciprocity

## The exchange of air

Reciprocity constitutes the central conceptual principle of AERA.

The object is not exclusively a provider of airflow, and the human subject is not exclusively its recipient.

Both participate in an exchange through which language and air circulate between them.

## Human to object

The human subject offers:

Language.

Thermal references.

Attention.

Presence.

Breath.

## Object to human

The object offers:

Airflow.

Physical sensation.

Linguistic interpretation.

Poetry.

An invitation to participate.

## Reversal of roles

Initially, the human subject speaks and the object responds physically.

During silence, the roles are reversed.

The object speaks and the human subject responds physically.

The object becomes the initiator of communication, while the human becomes the recipient of its linguistic expression.

## Breath as a reciprocal gesture

The breath is not understood as a command.

It is a material gesture through which the subject returns the same substance previously received from the object.

The exchange establishes a temporary condition of mutual dependence.

## From unilateral service to reciprocity

Conventional appliance vs ALLY
Command	vs Communication

Service	vs Exchange
Consumption	vs Reciprocity

## Conclusion

The fan gives air because the subject speaks.

The subject gives air because the fan speaks.

Language becomes airflow. Airflow becomes sensation. Silence becomes poetry. Poetry becomes breath.

Through this continuous exchange, the distinction between provider and recipient becomes unstable.

The human and the object become extensions of one another.
