
## Instructions for the human subject

Ally does not require conventional buttons or verbal commands. 
The interaction is established through language, silence and breath. 

1. Approach

Position yourself within conversational distance of the object. 

2. Speak

Speak naturally in English, Spanish or a combination of both languages. 
Your discourse may refer to physical heat, bodily sensations, environmental conditions, desire, memories, emotions or material transformations. 
You may speak, narrate, describe, read poetry or whisper. 

3. Experience

Observe how the object translates your language into movement and airflow. 
The greater the accumulation of heat-related references, the greater the intensity of the airflow. 
The object responds to the content of your speech rather than it'c volume. 

4. Remain Silent

Stop speaking and observe how the object gradually decreases its speed until it becomes still. 

5. Listen

During prolonged silence the object generates and recites a poem. 
The open expresses its own need for air, proximity and human breath. 

6. Reciprocate

Approach the designated microphone and gently exhale towards it without making physical contact. 
Return to the object the same material substance that it previously offered you: air. 

7. Reconnect

Once your breath has been recognized, the object acknowledges the exchange and returns to its listening state. 
The conversation can begin again.
