
Temporal behaviour

Description

The behaviour of Ally develops through time rather than through instantaneous commands.

Its movement follows a sequence of accumulation, intensification, saturation, dissipation, stillness and renewal.

## Temporal phases

Phase	           -  Description
## Phase 1
Accumulation: Heat-related words accumulate within a rolling time window.
## Phase 2
Intensification: The fan progressively increases its speed.
## Phase 3
Saturation: The fan reaches its maximum programmed speed.
## Phase 4
Dissipation: Thermal references expire and the fan decreases its speed.
## Phase 5
Stillness: The fan stops rotating.
## Phase 5
Invitation: The object generates and recites poetry.
## Phase 6
Waiting: The object awaits human breath.
## Phase 7
Reception: The object detects the reciprocal gesture.
## Phase 8
Renewal: The object returns to its listening state.

## Temporal parameters

Vocabulary accumulation: 15 seconds.

Silence before target speed becomes zero: 5 seconds.

Poetic invitation: after 20 seconds of silence and once the fan has stopped.

Minimum breath duration: 450 milliseconds.

Waiting for breath: indefinite.

Repeated invitation: approximately every 45 seconds.

## Design intention

The transitions between states should be gradual and organic.

The object should communicate through acceleration, deceleration, duration and absence rather than abrupt mechanical reactions.

Time becomes a material component of the interaction.

All numerical values are initial design parameters rather than measured performance.
