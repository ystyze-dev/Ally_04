
## Bilingual heat vocabulary

Objective

Develop an expanded linguistic database that allows AERA to interpret and express the multiple manifestations of heat.

The vocabulary extends beyond physical temperature to include emotional, erotic, environmental, atmospheric, bodily, material and symbolic associations.

## Languages

English.

Spanish.

## Grammatical organization

Each language contains seven JSON files:

Category	English	Spanish
Nouns	Nouns	Sustantivos
Verbs	Verbs	Verbos
Adjectives	Adjectives	Adjetivos
Adverbs	Adverbs	Adverbios
Prepositions	Prepositions	Preposiciones
Connectors	Connectors	Conectores
Interjections	Interjections	Interjecciones
Data structure

Each JSON file contains a single array of words or expressions.

Example:

["heat", "ardor", "desire", "wax", "sweat", "humidity"]

## Semantic dimensions

Heat as a physical phenomenon.

Heat as an environmental condition.

Heat as a bodily sensation.

Heat as emotional intensity.

Heat as erotic desire.

Heat as material transformation.

Heat as atmospheric moisture.

Heat as a symbolic condition.

## Computational applications

Recognition

Identify thermal references within human speech.

Generation

Construct bilingual poetry through randomized linguistic combinations and thematic patterns.

Physical translation

Convert the accumulation of recognized heat references into motor speed and airflow.

## Vocabulary maintenance

New words may be incorporated as the research develops.

Repeated entries should be avoided within each grammatical category.

Words that perform multiple grammatical functions may appear in more than one category.

General grammatical words support poetry generation but should not independently activate the fan.
