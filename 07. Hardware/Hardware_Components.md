## Computational components

Raspberry Pi power supply:	Computer power
USB microphone:	Speech and breath reception

## Mechanical and electrical components

Component	Function

Fan motor:	Rotational movement
Motor controller:	Speed regulation
External motor power supply:	Motor power
Four fan blades:	Airflow production
Protective grille:	Physical protection
Circular structural frame:	Mechanical support
Ceiling suspension:	Installation support
Electronic enclosure:	Component protection

## Optional components

Component	Function

Dedicated breath microphone: Improved exhalation detection
Airflow sensor:	Direct measurement of human breath
Proximity sensor:	Detection of subject approaching
Temperature sensor:	Environmental thermal measurement
Humidity sensor:	Atmospheric moisture measurement

note: only the microphone will be used in this project

## Material and finish

Sage-green structural frame.

Terracotta-colored blades.

Deep-green electronic display.

Ivory neutral surfaces.

Hardware requirements

The motor controller must be compatible with the selected motor's voltage, current and control method.

The Raspberry Pi must not directly power the motor.

The suspension system must support the complete assembly and withstand vibration during operation.

The interaction interface must remain safely accessible without requiring the subject to approach rotating blades.
