
Behavioral matrix

## Human input and object response

Human input	                              -  Object state	    -  Physical output	          -  Linguistic output
Unrelated speech	                        -  Listening	      -  Stillness or deceleration	-  None
Heat-related speech	                      -  Accumulation	    -  Low airflow	              -  None
Continued heat discourse	                -  Intensification	-  Medium airflow	            -  None
High concentration of thermal references	-  Saturation	      -  Maximum airflow	          -  None
End of heat discourse	                    -  Dissipation      -  Gradual deceleration	      -  None
Prolonged silence	                        -  Invitation	      -  Stillness	                -  Spoken heat poetry
Subject approaches	                      -  Waiting	        -  Stillness	                -  Displayed invitation
Human exhalation	                        -  Reception	      -  Breath detection	          -  Poetic acknowledgment
Absence of breath	                        -  Waiting	        -  Stillness	                -  Repeated invitation
Speech before breath is returned	        -  Waiting	        -  Stillness	                -  Reciprocal request remains active
Speech after reciprocal exchange	        -  Renewal	        -  Responsive airflow	        -  None


## Computational states

State	       -  Function
LISTEN	     -  Recognize speech and regulate airflow.
INVITE	     -  Generate and recite heat poetry.
WAIT_BREATH	 -  Wait for directed human exhalation.
ACK	         -  Acknowledge the breath and reset the interaction.

## Interaction condition

Speech alone does not complete the reciprocal exchange.

The object requires a bodily gesture in the form of human breath before returning to its normal listening state.
