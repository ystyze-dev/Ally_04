Your words will appear here.

Choose a language, then tap Start speaking.

Microphone access starts only when you press Start. Speech may be processed by your browser’s service; breath level is analyzed in this page, and audio or transcripts aren’t saved.

When Ally is still, blow gently toward the microphone. The browser demo estimates airflow from sound level; other sustained sounds may also trigger it.

After speech, the breeze fades to stillness over five seconds. A poem arrives after twenty seconds of silence. Browser speech support varies; no physical fan or breath sensor is connected.

The reciprocal cycle

Air changes hands.

Not a command, but a conversation: language becomes movement; silence becomes an invitation.

Ally is an experimental, ceiling-suspended fan concept: four terracotta blades within a sage-green protective structure, with a small display and microphone below. Its interface is being explored as a bridge between functional object and relational presence.
