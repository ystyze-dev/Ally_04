## Interaction flowchart

## Objective

Describe the computational and relational sequence through which AERA establishes communication with the human subject.

## flowchart TD
    A[Human subject] --> B[Speech]
    B --> C[Speech recognition]
    C --> D[Bilingual heat vocabulary]
    D --> E{Heat references?}
    E -->|Yes| F[Increase airflow]
    E -->|No| G[Decrease airflow]
    F --> H{Prolonged silence?}
    G --> H
    H -->|No| B
    H -->|Yes| I[Stillness]
    I --> J[Generate heat poetry]
    J --> K[Request human breath]
    K --> L{Breath received?}
    L -->|No| M[Wait]
    M --> K
    L -->|Yes| N[Acknowledge breath]
    N --> O[Reciprocity]
    O --> B
## Interaction principle

The object alternates between receptive and expressive states.

During the receptive state, human language becomes airflow.

During the expressive state, the object's language becomes an invitation for human breath.

The cycle is completed through the reciprocal exchange of air.
